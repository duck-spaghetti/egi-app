# EGI favorites

Personal selection of public services for later consultation.

## Language

**Favorite**:
A public service selected by a visitor and kept in their personal list on a device.
_Avoid_: Subscription, account bookmark

**Saved card**:
The copy of a favorite service's public information at its last successful update.
It is not a claim that the service is currently available.

**Unavailable favorite**:
An entry whose service is no longer publicly readable after a successful check;
its old service details have been removed from the device.

- **Collection / lista**: a named local set of public service copies, independently
  managed from quick favorites and other collections. “Le tue liste” is its UI.
- **Membership / appartenenza**: one deduplicated service copy inside a collection;
  its saved timestamp/language and withdrawal state remain explicit.
